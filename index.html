The Manifesto

Svartsalt fuses blackened death metal aggression, thrash riffs, and heavy post-metal structures into a sonic confrontation with a dark era. Writing exclusively in Faroese, our lyrics target political corruption, religious fundamentalism, fascist tendencies, and environmental destruction — standing as an unyielding voice rising from the underground.
