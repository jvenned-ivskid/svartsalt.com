Manifesto

Svartsalt fuses blackened death metal aggression, thrash riffs, and heavy post-metal structures into a sonic confrontation with a dark era.

Writing exclusively in Faroese, our lyrics target political corruption, religious fundamentalism, fascist tendencies, and environmental destruction — standing as an unyielding voice rising from the deep.

Svartsalt sameinir myrkan death metal agressjón, thrash-riffs og tung post-metal strubturar í einum soniskum samsteypi við eina myrka tíðarfestu.

Við tekstum bert á føroyskum, siktar tónleikurin beint mótvegis politiskari korruptsjón, religiøsum fundamentalismu, faskistiskum tendensum og umhvørvisoyðilegging — sum ein óvíkjandi rødd úr dýpinum.
